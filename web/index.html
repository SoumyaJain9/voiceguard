Acoustic Biomarkers & Explainability Matrix

📈

Jitter (Pitch Stability)

Measures cycle-to-cycle variations in fundamental frequency (F0). AI vocoders often produce unnaturally static pitch trajectories or erratic synthetic glitches.

🔊

Shimmer (Amplitude Dynamics)

Quantifies micro-variations in waveform peak amplitudes. Human speech contains natural breathiness and emotional modulation absent in synthetic models.

🎼

Harmonics-to-Noise (HNR)

Evaluates harmonic vocal tract energy versus spectral noise floor. Vocoder synthesis artifacts and neural phase dispersion trigger low HNR values (< 20 dB).

🕸️

Spectro-Temporal GAT

AASIST Graph Attention Networks treat time and frequency bins as graph nodes, uncovering subtle non-local spectral phase artifacts left behind by generative models.
